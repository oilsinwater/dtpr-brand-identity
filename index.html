Three sections

Each section answers a different question. Read straight through if you are new to DTPR. Jump to the section that answers your question if you are returning.

OBSERVED

Section 1 · Brand Audit

An inventory of the DTPR visual identity as it exists in the wild — across dtpr.io, vision.dtpr.io, makeshift2026.dtpr.io, docs.dtpr.io, the Assembly Day deck, the Message Map, and MakeShift print assets.

What it is: observation, screenshots, source-code citations, analysis.

What it is not: prescriptive rules. Decisions and color-token lockups are observed or inferred, never invented.

Section 2 · Visual System Prep

A structured set of rules and parameters defining how DTPR assets behave. Morphological parameter boxes (Form, Process, Semantic), Sprint Recipes (Regulatory, Maker, Commons), honeycomb blueprinting, ISO 3864 check, responsive specifications.

What it is: the prescriptive engine that dictates how symbols, layouts, and warning matrices scale in the field.

What it is not: static brand history.

Section 3 · Brand Identity Guidelines

A prescriptive reference for how the DTPR identity is built and used — values, logo, color, type, photography, and visual language in one place. Authoritative for color and fonts going forward; supersedes the audit's color and typography pages.

What it is: the rules for the identity.

What it is not: observation (Section 1) or parameters (Section 2).

How to read this brand book

Three sections, three questions, three readers.

Section Question it answers Reader
1 · Brand Audit What does the DTPR identity actually look like in the wild? New designers, stakeholders, reviewers, anyone asking "where did the brand come from?"
2 · Visual System Prep How do the system variables combine into recipes? Designers and engineers building new DTPR surfaces; contributors customizing for a specific deployment.
3 · Brand Identity Guidelines How do I use the identity correctly? Anyone deploying, presenting, or extending the DTPR identity. This is the section most readers will spend the most time in.

Sources

The three sections draw on the same sources — live sites, Assembly Day deck, Message Map, MakeShift print collateral, the Superbloom design corpus, and the Recipe B locked direction. The audit cites them; the visual system prep uses them as input; the guidelines commit to them.
