DTPR
Brand Book

Digital Trust for Places & Routines. The complete narrative of the DTPR identity, in two sections — observation and rules.

StewardHelpful Places · CC BY 4.0
AuthorPhilliph Drummond, Senior Tech Design Researcher · Superbloom Design · August 2026
What this is. A single narrative with two sections. Each section is independently useful; together they form a complete identity reference. Section 1 documents what exists in the wild. Section 2 documents the rules for using the identity.

Two sections

Each section answers a different question. Read straight through if you are new to DTPR. Jump to the section that answers your question if you are returning.

Section 1 · Brand Audit →

An inventory of the DTPR visual identity as it exists in the wild — across dtpr.io, vision.dtpr.io, makeshift2026.dtpr.io, docs.dtpr.io, the Assembly Day deck, the Message Map, and MakeShift print assets.

What it is: observation, screenshots, source-code citations, analysis.

What it is not: prescriptive rules. Decisions and color-token lockups are observed or inferred, never invented.

Pages: 12 (01 Evolution → 12 Findings).

Section 2 · Brand Identity Guidelines →

A prescriptive reference for how the DTPR identity is built and used — values, logo, color, type, photography, and visual language in one place. Authoritative for color and fonts going forward; supersedes the audit's color and typography pages.

What it is: the rules for the identity.

What it is not: observation (Section 1).

Pages: 11 (Cover + 10 content).

How to read this brand identity book

Two sections, two questions, two readers.

Section Question it answers Reader
1 · Brand Audit What does the DTPR identity actually look like in the wild? New designers, stakeholders, reviewers, anyone asking "where did the brand identity come from?"
2 · Brand Identity Guidelines How do I use the identity correctly? Anyone deploying, presenting, or extending the DTPR identity. This is the section most readers will spend the most time in.

Sources

The two sections draw on the same sources — live sites, Assembly Day deck, Message Map, MakeShift print collateral, the Superbloom design corpus, and the Recipe B locked direction. The audit cites them; the guidelines commit to them.
