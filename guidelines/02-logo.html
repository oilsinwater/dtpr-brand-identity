The DTPR mark and the canonical lockup

The hexagon is the brand's atomic unit. The canonical mark is a hexagon with an inner circle and a small offset square. Three SVG variants live in the assets directory: blue solid (default), black solid, and outline. The wordmark lockup pairs the mark with the letters "DTPR" in Public Sans Bold.

Geometry breakdown

Canonical marks are solid hex + white inner circle + colored square. Lockups: navy and black variants keep the white inner circle; the white variant omits the inner circle so the host surface shows through directly as a circle.

Canonical wordmark lockup

The mark + wordmark lockup. Public Sans Bold, navy #002684, paired with the navy-solid mark. Default horizontal lockup. SVG sources: assets/marks/dtpr_logo_horizontal_<color>.svg for navy and black; assets/marks/dtpr_logo_horizontal_white_on_<bg>.svg for white-on-navy and white-on-black.

Black solid (on white)
White solid (on black)

Lockup rules

  • Wordmark is always Public Sans Bold (or Bold 700 weight equivalent). Never another weight.
  • Wordmark is always the same color as the mark. Navy mark + navy wordmark, black mark + black wordmark, white mark + white wordmark.
  • Lockups: navy and black variants keep the white inner circle. White variants use a bg-colored inner circle (navy for white-on-navy, black for white-on-black) with a white square on top, so both the circle and the square are visible against the host surface.
  • Letter-spacing: -0.01em. The wordmark is tight, not loose.
  • Mark-to-wordmark gap: 0.6rem. The mark sits at the cap height of the wordmark.
  • No alternate font pairings. No italic. No script. No all-caps with tracking.

When to use which variant

Recommended
  • Navy solid (default) — for headers, footers, official documents, signage.
  • Black solid — for print, single-color reproduction, fax.
  • Outline — for dark or busy backgrounds where solid would disappear.
  • Inverted (white on dark / dark on light) — for footer bars, dark chrome, hero overlays.
Avoid
  • Recoloring the mark in arbitrary hues (chartreuse, salmon, coral are reserved for status/accent only).
  • Drop shadows, bevels, or 3D effects.
  • Containerizing the mark in shapes that fight its geometry.
  • Using the layered hex (event-specific) on a non-event surface.
