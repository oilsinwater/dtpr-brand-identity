05 · Icon

The icon, in five treatments

The icon is the DTPR mark alone, without the wordmark. Five treatments cover every context where the mark appears by itself — favicons, app icons, button glyphs, certifications, and partner co-branding lockups.

OBSERVED PROPOSED

The five treatments

01 · Brand colors
Navy solid #002684
02 · Inverted
White on navy
03 · Black & white
Outline, no fill
04 · Monochrome
Black solid #000000
05 · Inverse monochrome
White solid on dark

When to use each

Treatment Use when
Brand colors Default — anywhere a navy mark is appropriate.
Inverted On navy or dark backgrounds. White-on-navy is the primary chrome treatment.
Black & white Print, fax, single-color reproduction where outline reads better than solid.
Monochrome On light backgrounds where pure black reads as authoritative (legal, certification).
Inverse monochrome On dark backgrounds where the inverse treatment feels heavier than outline.

Forbidden modifications

Recommended
  • Use the canonical navy solid by default.
  • Switch to inverted for dark backgrounds.
  • Maintain 0.4× mark-height clearspace on all sides (see 06 Dimensions).
  • Pair with a single wordmark when used as a lockup (see 02 Logo).
Avoid
  • Recoloring the icon in chartreuse, salmon, coral, or any accent color — those are reserved.
  • Adding gradients, drop shadows, bevels, or 3D effects.
  • Rotating the icon or modifying its hexagonal geometry.
  • Combining the icon with a partner logo without a clear divider or spacing rule.
