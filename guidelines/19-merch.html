19 · Merch & Swag

Merch and swag that holds the line.

DTPR Merch & Swag — tees, totes, stickers, pins, patches. Apparel and accessories that steward the brand identity without cheapening it.

MERCHPRINT-ON-DEMANDSTEWARDSHIP

Tee

Heavyweight cotton, white surface, single-color navy stacked lockup on the chest, sized to read from 3 ft away. No back print.

Tee · white cotton · stacked lockup chest print · no back print

Tote bag

Natural canvas tote, 14×16 in. Single-color navy stacked lockup screen print centered on the front panel. Web handles, no gusset. Pairs with the workshop kit.

Tote · natural canvas · 14×16 in · stacked lockup single-color print

Sticker

Round die-cut, 3 in diameter. Stacked lockup centered on white surface, navy print.

Sticker · 3 in round · white surface · navy stacked lockup · die-cut

Button / pin

1.25 in round pin-back button. Medium-blue surface — secondary blue is the merch accent; the steward's mark lives on a colored field so it reads as a badge, not a brand element. White stacked lockup at center.

Button/pin · 1.25 in · medium-blue surface · stacked lockup in white · steward's mark
