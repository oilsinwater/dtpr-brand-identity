Fostering digital trust for smarter places.

Sydney Olympic Park is advancing a model of responsible innovation. With DTPR, the Park introduced transparent communication about its use of CCTV, public WiFi, crowd measurement, and smart irrigation systems — establishing a new standard for accountability and public trust in urban technology.
