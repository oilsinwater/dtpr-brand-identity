VISION // A DAY WITH DTPR

A journey that rejects the false dichotomy between privacy and innovation.

Out of the co-design sessions in early 2020 came an ask: envision how the standard might work in five years. Excitement built for the prospect of a personal AI assistant — provided by an organisation you trust, knowing your preferences, ready to help.

01 // FIRST, CHOOSE YOUR AGENT

In your everyday life you exchange data with companies, apps, shops and people. Everyone has a different level of comfort sharing data depending on the situation, and we believe control improves comfort. So these scenarios imagine a world where everyone has their own digital personal assistant.

Who would you trust to hold it?

02 // SEVEN SCENARIOSHow might we…

Each scenario opens with a question the co-design sessions kept returning to, then walks a handful of ordinary moments where a system has to declare itself before it takes anything.
