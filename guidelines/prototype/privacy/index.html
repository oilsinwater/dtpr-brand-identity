Privacy Policy

Our commitment to protecting your privacy and personal information.

Last updated: 26 October 2025

This Privacy Notice describes how DTPR protects the privacy of visitors to our website at dtpr.io (the “Website”).

Transparency is core to who we are as an organisation, and we have done our best to make this notice easy to read and to let you find what you are looking for. If you would like any more information, reach out at hello@dtpr.io.

In short

Unlike most websites, we collect no personal information about you for tracking or analytics. We use Plausible, a privacy-friendly open-source analytics tool that uses no cookies and no tracking technology.

How does DTPR use my personal information?

To respond to your requests, questions and feedback

When you contact us through our contact form, we collect your email address and the content of your message. We use this to respond to your enquiry and communicate with you about your questions or feedback.

Does DTPR collect any other data?

We monitor use of the Website and collect aggregated, non-personal statistics to understand how visitors engage with our content and to improve it. This data cannot be used to identify individual visitors.

Does DTPR sell or share my personal information?

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with the service providers who help us operate — and that list is published in full in the complete notice.

How does DTPR protect my personal information?

We apply administrative, technical and physical safeguards appropriate to the small amount of information we hold, and we retain it only as long as needed to answer you.

What are my rights with regard to privacy?

To access and correct your personal information

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Write to us and we will action it.

To lodge a complaint (Canada)

If you are not satisfied with how we have handled your information, you may lodge a complaint with the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

How do I get in touch?

Questions, requests, or complaints about this notice can go to hello@dtpr.io.
