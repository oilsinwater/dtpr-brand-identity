Privacy Policy

Digital Trust for Places and Routines (DTPR) is committed to radical digital transparency, privacy-by-design, and open governance.

1. Zero Tracking & No Cookies

This website does not use tracking cookies, persistent identifiers, advertising pixels, or third-party behavioral profiling scripts. We believe an open standard for public space digital transparency must lead by example on the web.

2. Open Source Standard & Licensing

DTPR taxonomy schemas, iconography, signage templates, and implementation guides are published under open permissive licenses:

  • Design System & Visual Marks: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)
  • Taxonomy Schemas & Code: Apache 2.0 / MIT License

3. Stewardship & Data Autonomy

DTPR is an open standard stewarded by Superbloom Design (formerly Helpful Places). DTPR empowers residents and municipal leaders to hold digital deployments accountable. The standard does not collect or centralize public surveillance feeds.

4. Contact & Community Governance

For inquiries regarding the standard, privacy practices, or contributing to the taxonomy, visit our repository at github.com/dtpr/dtpr-standard or contact the stewardship team at superbloom.design.
