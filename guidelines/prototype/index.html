You can't trust a technology you can't understand.

Every day, invisible systems make decisions about our cities, our data, and our lives.

THE DILEMMAHow DTPR Solves This→

Technology increasingly influences our lives, but in ways that are invisible and hard to see. Without the ability to understand how it works, people become distrustful of these systems and the organizations that use them.

That invisibility is a problem. Whether it's a traffic camera or an AI decision maker, people deserve a say in how these technologies get deployed. Unfortunately, legalese and tech speak make being transparent almost impossible.

A common language for every public space.

Whether in a transit dock, a public plaza, or a civic library, DTPR ensures technology declares itself before it collects data. By establishing upfront disclosures for purpose, accountable entities, and data flows, the standard turns complex digital systems into clear, verifiable public infrastructure.
