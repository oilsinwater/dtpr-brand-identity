You can't trust a technology you can't understand.

Every day, invisible systems make decisions about our cities, our data, and our lives.

The Invisibility ProblemHow DTPR Solves This→

Technology increasingly influences our lives, but in ways that are invisible and hard to see. Without the ability to understand how it works, people become distrustful of these systems and the organizations that use them.

That invisibility is a problem. Whether it's a traffic camera or an AI decision maker, people deserve a say in how these technologies get deployed. DTPR creates open, standardized communication channels between technology deployers and the public.
