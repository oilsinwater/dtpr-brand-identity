01 // THE PROBLEM
THE CHALLENGE

You Can’t Trust a Technology You Can’t Understand.

Every day, invisible systems make decisions about our cities, our data, and our lives.

How DTPR Solves This→

Technology increasingly influences our lives, but in ways that are invisible and hard to see. Without the ability to understand how it works, people become distrustful of these systems and the organizations that use them. So why is it that most attempts to explain how these systems function rely on an engineering degree or ten thousand pages of legalese?

That invisibility is a problem. Whether it’s a traffic camera or an AI decision maker, people deserve a say in how these technologies get deployed. Unfortunately, legalese and tech speak make being transparent almost impossible. Seriously, when’s the last time you read a terms of service agreement?

DTPR is an open standard that gives people a common language for how technology uses data to impact their lives. The standard combines three components: a taxonomy of technology concepts and uses; a design system (visual icons and common language); and data chains which are structured ways to present detailed information about a specific technology.

With this combination, organizations can communicate both inside and out about how data is collected, generated, and used.

A common language for every public space.

Whether in a transit dock, a public plaza, or a civic library, DTPR ensures technology declares itself before it collects data. By establishing upfront disclosures for purpose, accountable entities, and data flows, the standard turns complex digital systems into clear, verifiable public infrastructure.
