16 · Print Collateral

Print collateral.

DTPR Print Collateral — one-pagers, brochures, posters, reports. Token-driven applications of the brand across printed surfaces.

PRINTONE-PAGERBROCHUREPOSTER

One-pager

Letter-format (8.5×11 in / 210×297 mm) fact sheet or summary document. Masthead + display headline + standfirst + two-column body + footer. Used for stakeholder briefings, council memos, and event handouts.

DTPR · Standard Brief

DOC-001 · v2.1

A standard for accountable technology.

DTPR translates the invisible systems that operate in public space — sensors, cameras, AI decision-makers — into legible disclosures that a person walking past can read.

The standard combines a taxonomy of concepts, a visual system of icons, and auditable data chains. Deployments name the accountable steward, declare the primary purpose, and disclose what is collected and where it goes.

Six years in the field. Fifteen communities on three continents. Published under CC BY 4.0. Stewarded by Helpful Places; designed in collaboration with Superbloom.

This brief is one of three formats. A one-pager is the standard. A brochure extends it to three folds for events. A poster is the public-space variant.

Poster

Vertical 2:3 format for civic-space display. The chartreuse dot appears only on the footer line (active deployment indicator). White surface, navy text.

This technology is accountable to you.

Every deployment declares who runs it, what it does, and where the data goes. The disclosure is physical, public, and standardized — readable in passing.

DTPR is an open standard published under CC BY 4.0. Anyone can use it, adapt it, or build on it without permission or fee.
