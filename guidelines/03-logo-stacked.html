03 · Logo (Stacked)

The stacked lockup

The stacked lockup places the mark above the wordmark, vertically centered. Use it for square containers, vertical layouts, app icons, social media avatars, certification stamps, and any context where the horizontal lockup would be cramped or visually unbalanced.

PROPOSED

Stacked lockup — three variants

Navy solid (default)
Black solid
White (on navy)

Stacked lockup rules

  • Mark sits above the wordmark, vertically centered along the wordmark's optical axis.
  • Mark-to-wordmark gap: 0.75rem (slightly larger than horizontal lockup's 0.6rem, to compensate for vertical reading).
  • Wordmark is centered horizontally with the mark — not left-aligned.
  • The stacked lockup is intended for square or near-square containers. For wide horizontal layouts, use the horizontal lockup instead.
  • Minimum size: 48px on the mark dimension (smaller than horizontal because the lockup needs vertical room to breathe).

When to use which

Stacked is for
  • Square containers (1:1 app icons, social avatars, favicons).
  • Vertical layouts (slide sidebars, document spines, signage).
  • Hero treatments where the stacked brand mark gets its own column.
  • Certification stamps, badges, and seal-like contexts.
Horizontal is for
  • Headers (where the horizontal logo lockup sits in a wide navigation bar).
  • Footers (where horizontal balance matches the layout).
  • Email signatures and letterhead.
  • Any context where the available width exceeds the available height.
