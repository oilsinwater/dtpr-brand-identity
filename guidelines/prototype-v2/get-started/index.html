GUIDE // GETTING STARTED

What is DTPR, and how does it work?

DTPR is a standard for describing how technologies work in a clear, accessible way. Rather than relying on dense technical specifications or abstract terms of service, it translates those layers into simple language and visual iconography.

01 // THE SHAPE OF ITThree concepts

DTPR highlights the key questions our community research has shown people need answered to feel they can trust how a technology is being used. It works by combining three core concepts.

01

A taxonomy

A structured set of technology concepts, grouped by the questions people actually ask about them.

02

A visual language

Open-source icons and plain-language descriptions, plus guidance on how to arrange them.

03

A feedback channel

A short survey so residents can respond to how a technology is being used, not just read about it.

02 // THE TAXONOMY

Grouped concepts, not a glossary.

A taxonomy is a collection of concepts grouped into categories by shared traits. When scientists describe animals they don't start from scratch each time — they ask whether it has fur, how many legs it has, and each answer narrows the field.

DTPR works the same way. We scoured the academic literature, hosted public workshops, and talked with community members to understand their concerns. What were they asking? What answers did they wish they had, or feel were being hidden? Then we grouped those questions into categories.

COMMON TO ALL TECHNOLOGY6 QUESTIONS
  • accountableWho is accountable for this technology?
  • purposeWhat is the purpose of this technology?
  • collectedWhat type of data is being collected?
  • processedHow will this data be processed?
  • accessWho can access this data?
  • storedHow is this data stored?

IF IT'S A DEVICE

Cameras, air quality sensors, wifi routers — one further question: what type of technology is this?

IF IT'S AN ALGORITHM

  • How does this program function?
  • What are the risks, and how are they mitigated?
  • What rights do I have in relation to it?
  • What data goes in, and what comes out?
  • What kind of algorithm is being used?

03 // THE VISUAL LANGUAGE

Think about nutrition labels. Nutritional science is hard to understand, and if every item in the grocery store came with a ten-page report, running errands would be impossible. What nutrition labels do is give a simplified, consistent layout for the most important information.

DTPR does the same. For each taxonomy category it provides open-source icons and plain-language descriptions, plus clear guidance on how to arrange them.

04 // THE FEEDBACK CHANNELACCOUNTABILITY GOES BOTH WAYS

Disclosure without a reply is just a notice.

For people to trust a technology they need more than information. They need a voice.

That people need to affect a technology's use, not just read about it, has been a key theme in nearly every piece of community research DTPR has conducted.

So we have put together a brief survey that can be attached to any DTPR instance. Seven questions let residents rate a technology from 1 to 5 on trustworthiness, straightforwardness, and strength of benefit.
