06 · Dimensions & Clear Space

How much room the identity needs

Every mark has a minimum size below which it becomes unreadable, and a clearspace around which no other element may intrude. The DTPR mark is no exception. These rules protect the mark's legibility across every surface.

PROPOSED

The clearspace rule

Maintain a clearspace of 0.4× the mark's height on all sides. No other element — text, image, container edge — may enter this space.

The dashed chartreuse border shows the 0.4× mark-height clearspace on all four sides.

Why 0.4× and not 0.5× or 1×

The DTPR mark is geometrically compact (hexagon + inner circle + small offset square). 0.4× is the smallest ratio that still reads as "protected zone" without crowding the mark. Smaller ratios (0.2×, 0.3×) cause the mark to visually merge with adjacent elements. Larger ratios (0.5×, 1×) waste layout real estate on surfaces where space is at a premium (signage, mobile).

Minimum sizes

Surface Mark height Notes
Digital · favicon 16 × 16px Inner circle and offset square collapse below 16px. Below this, use the mark without the inner details.
Digital · header / footer 24 × 24px minimum Recommended 32 × 32px for primary chrome.
Digital · app icon 48 × 48px minimum PWA / mobile app store requirements often exceed this.
Print · business card 8mm Reproduce at 600dpi minimum.
Print · letterhead / document 12mm Standard for official documents.
Signage · outdoor 30mm at viewing distance Scale up 1.5× for every additional 5m of viewing distance.

Forbidden modifications

Recommended
  • Maintain the 0.4× clearspace rule on all four sides.
  • Respect minimum sizes for the surface.
  • Scale the mark proportionally — never stretch or distort.
  • Use the appropriate variant for the surface (see 05 Icon).
Avoid
  • Crowding the mark with text, other marks, or container edges.
  • Placing the mark on busy photographic backgrounds without sufficient contrast.
  • Rotating the mark off its hexagonal axis.
  • Adding outlines, shadows, bevels, or 3D effects to the mark.
  • Reproducing the mark at sizes below the minimum for the surface.

OPEN: signage viewing distance formula. The 1.5×-per-5m scaling rule above is PROPOSED. It needs validation against existing signage assets in the field before becoming a locked rule.
