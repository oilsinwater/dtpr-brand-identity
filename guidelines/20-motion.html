20 · Motion

Motion, in service of clarity.

Motion principles

  1. Quiet over flashy. Motion should inform, not perform. Cross-fades over wipe-ins; fades over rotations.
  2. Chartreuse animates only the live signal. The pulse on the recipe-live-dot is the only place chartreuse animates. Hover states on chartreuse CTAs deepen the color, not glow.
  3. Honor prefers-reduced-motion. Every animated element on the site respects prefers-reduced-motion: reduce and stops. The pulse, the screen-swap, the city-card hover — all of them.
  4. Durations under 250ms. Transitions are fast. Anything longer reads as "broken" or "loading."
  5. Translate, never bounce. No spring physics on civic-tech content. The movements are linear or ease-out, not overshoot.
